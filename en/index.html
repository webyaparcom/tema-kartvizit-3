Frequently asked questions
$Where are your columns published?keyboard_arrow_down

In a weekly Sunday supplement and in this site’s archive. The outlet and columns are fictional.

$Do you answer reader letters?keyboard_arrow_down

Yes, within two weeks. Letters never appear in a column without your consent.

$Do I need writing experience to join a workshop?keyboard_arrow_down

No. A notebook and a pen are enough; we write a short memory in the first session.

$May I quote your columns?keyboard_arrow_down

Short quotes may be used with credit; write to me for the full text.
